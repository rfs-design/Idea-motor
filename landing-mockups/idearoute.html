IdeaRoute

“From idea to route.” A spoken idea, charted turn-by-turn into the operational path that gets it made.

Words by the engine · Voice → AI workflow · Reading time: as long as it takes to speak your idea aloud. IdeaRoute doesn't map your mind — it plots the route your idea should travel.

You have the idea already. What you don't have — not yet — is the road it takes to become real: which tool first, what to ask it, what to carry into the next stop. That gap is where most ideas stall.

IdeaRoute closes it. Speak the idea in your own words and it recognises the creative archetype underneath — a campaign, a film, a deck, a piece of AI graphics — then draws the route to match. Not a mind-map of loose bubbles, but a sequence with a direction.

Each waypoint names the right instrument — Perplexity to survey the terrain, Claude to shape the concept, Midjourney to set the look — and hands you a dense, paste-ready prompt written for this idea, not a generic one.

The result reads like a field guide to your own project: turn here, ask this, expect that. You bring the vision; IdeaRoute brings the map that respects it.

It doesn't map your mind. It plots the route.

The route, in four stages
01
Input · voice

Speak the idea

Say it out loud, messy and unedited. Your voice becomes the brief — the lowest-friction on-ramp there is.

02
Engine · Gemini

Read the terrain

The archetype is recognised — web, comms, AI graphics, deck, spot, research — and the route is chosen to fit it.

03
Orchestration

Plot the waypoints

A multi-tool workflow is drawn end to end, each stop feeding the next with its own paste-ready prompt.

04
Output · prompts

Arrive

Copy the prompts, follow the route, and take the idea all the way to production.

Keep the route open
